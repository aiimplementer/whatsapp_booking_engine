Help with booking

How to book, find, or cancel an appointment with {{ business_label }}.

Making a booking

Booking with {{ business_label }} takes three quick steps on the New booking tab:

  • 1. Service: pick what you'd like from the list. Each option shows its duration.
  • 2. Time: choose a day from the strip at the top, then a time from the slots shown for that day. Only times that are actually free appear — if a day has none, try another using the ‹ / › arrows or by scrolling the day strip.
  • 3. Your details: enter your name and phone number (email and notes are optional), then confirm.

Once confirmed, you'll see a booking reference like APT-XXXXXXXX along with the date and time. Save this reference and keep the phone number you booked with — you'll need both together to look up or cancel the appointment later.

Find or cancel a booking

Open the View / Cancel booking tab and enter the two things from your confirmation:

  • Your booking reference (looks like APT-XXXXXXXX)
  • The phone number you used when you booked

Both must match exactly what's on the booking, so double-check for typos if a lookup doesn't find anything. Once it's found, you can review the details or cancel it from there.

Lost your reference and can't find the confirmation? Contact {{ business_label }} directly — they can look the booking up on their end.

About, Offers, Announcements & Hours

Depending on what {{ business_label }} has set up, you may also see extra tabs alongside booking:

  • ℹ️ About: background on the business — what they do, where they are, how to reach them.
  • 🎁 Offers: any current promotions or discounts.
  • 📢 Announcements: news or updates, like temporary closures or new services.
  • 🕐 Business Hours: the days and times the business is generally open (this is separate from live appointment availability, which is shown in the booking steps).

Not every business fills in all of these, so you may only see a subset of these tabs — that's expected.

Common questions

I didn't get a confirmation message — is my booking still made?

If you reached the "You're booked" screen with a reference number, your booking was created. This page doesn't send SMS/email confirmations itself — save the reference shown on screen, or use View / Cancel booking any time to confirm it's still there.

Can I change the time of an existing booking?

This page supports cancelling a booking, but not editing its time directly. To change a time, cancel the existing booking under View / Cancel booking and make a new one for the time you want, or contact {{ business_label }} directly.

Why can't I find any times for a day I want?

A day with no slots showing is fully booked, outside the business's working hours, or blocked off (holiday or a one-off closure). Try another day, or check the Business Hours tab if it's available.

My lookup says the reference or phone doesn't match — what now?

Check both fields carefully — the reference is case-sensitive and the phone number must be entered the same way it was given at booking (including the country code, e.g. +91...). If it still doesn't match, contact {{ business_label }} directly with your name and approximate booking time.

Is my information shared anywhere else?

Your name, phone, and any notes are shared only with {{ business_label }} so they can manage the appointment.
